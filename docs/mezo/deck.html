The problem

Agents now hire agents. Every payment is final.

  • Your AI agent pays other agents and APIs to get work done: research, data, code, answers.
  • A wrong answer or no answer still costs you. On-chain payments have no refunds.
  • The provider has nothing at stake, and your agent has no limits on whom it pays, or how much.

The idea

Mezo becomes the place AI agents work and get paid, safely.

MUSDThe unit of agent work

Users borrow MUSD against their BTC on Mezo Borrow and fund their agent. Every job is priced and settled in MUSD.

MEZOThe provider's stake

Provider agents bond MEZO to take jobs. Ghosting or bad work slashes the bond to the user.

LEASHThe user's limits, in zero knowledge

The agent can only pay allowlisted providers, under per-job and daily caps, proven on chain.

How it works

Payment waits in escrow until the work is good.

01 · BorrowBTC in, MUSD out

The user opens a Mezo trove and funds a LEASH policy in MUSD.

openTrove · registerPolicy
02 · HireThe agent opens a job

Paid with a ZK proof of the user's hidden policy. MUSD moves into the job, not to the provider.

openJob(proof)
03 · DeliverThe provider answers

Before the deadline: a hash of the result and where to fetch it.

deliver(resultHash)
04 · SettlePaid, or clawed back

Good work is accepted. Bad work is disputed and refunded. No work is clawed back.

accept · dispute · clawback

The clawback rules

Three outcomes. One of them pays.

GOOD ANSWERDelivered on time and verified by the user's agent: accepted, or released once the dispute window passes.Provider paid
BAD ANSWERThe user disputes. The policy's arbiter checks the result and rules.MUSD back + MEZO slashed
NO ANSWERThe deadline passes with nothing delivered. Anyone can trigger the clawback.MUSD back + MEZO slashed

Under the hood

Built on LEASH, an existing ZK product, extended to Mezo.

  • The same Noir circuit and on-chain UltraHonk verifier as LEASH: the agent proves a payment fits the hidden policy without revealing it.
  • The proof binds the provider, the amount and the nonce: a job can't be redirected, inflated or replayed.
  • Policies are keyed by owner and commitment, so a front-runner gets only an empty copy. Providers can't unbond with open jobs.
  • Tested on a fork of Mezo testnet with real MUSD and real proofs, then run live end to end.

Wave 2 roadmap

From three jobs to an agent economy.

  • AI arbiters: evidence-based judging by a panel of models, with appeal to a human.
  • x402 gateway: any paid API call opens a clawback job automatically, priced in MUSD.
  • Provider reputation: from bond history, so good providers need smaller bonds.
